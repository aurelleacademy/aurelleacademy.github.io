Frequently Asked Questions

What does an Aurelle Academy certificate signify?

An Aurelle Academy certificate attests that the graduate has successfully mastered our curriculum, completed hands-on clinical training, and adhered to our strict sanitation and execution standards. It is issued directly by our private academy as a mark of practical competence.

How does the online verification system work?

Each certificate features a scannable QR code pointing directly to our verification portal alongside an alphanumeric ID (e.g., AUR-J2GA-U9PB). Entering this code retrieves the candidate's verified name, course title, and completion date from an encrypted ledger.

Is prior aesthetic experience required to enroll?

Foundational tracks like Lash Application and Russian Manicure are designed for both ambitious beginners and practicing stylists seeking precision. Advanced modules such as 3D Brow Cover-Up and Clinical Micropigmentation require preliminary consultation to confirm manual readiness.

What hygiene and safety protocols are taught?

We practice hospital-grade aseptic techniques. Students learn the handling of single-use ethylene-oxide sterilized cartridge needles, medical autoclave protocols, bloodborne pathogen awareness, and safe hazardous waste management.
